# Changelog

## 1.3.0-rc1 — 2026-11-25
- HQ rebuilt as a real isometric game scene: one empty tower shell (Gemini) + 39 separate furniture sprites
  (Gemini asset sheets, chroma-keyed) placed on a measured floor grid with proper depth sorting.
- NPCs walk with A* around furniture, ride a working glass lift between levels and cross glass skybridges
  between towers (no more teleporting); the mezzanine slab and railing correctly cover the lower level.
- Movement, lift and life logic run on real time, independent of the frame rate.

## 1.2.0-rc1 — 2026-11-25
- HQ redesigned to match the reference art: three painted two-level towers (generated by the owner with Gemini),
  cut out and placed in a full-screen neon city with flying cars in front, behind and inside the windows.
- Living overlays on the painting: flickering screens, server LEDs, neon, steam, hologram glow, rain on glass,
  day/night window light, alarm beacons and smoke per room.
- NPCs (PixelLab): seated typing at their own desks, idle breathing, coffee, celebrate (job success),
  frustrated (job failure), talking (chats/hand-offs), yawning (night/idle); lift-teleport between floors.
- Office cat (PixelLab): wanders the lounge, grooms and sleeps.

## 1.1.0-rc1 — 2026-11-25
- New main UI "NEONLOOM HQ": living isometric pixel-art office (Phaser 3, served locally). Every feature lives in a room;
  no menus or settings buttons on screen (classic UI kept at /dashboard, key `).
- 10 NPC agents with new names (KAI-07, RAZE-11, JUNO-X9, VOLT-3, NYX-21, HEX-04, SABLE-6, ORIN-88, KADE-12, ZERO-1),
  sprites generated with PixelLab (8 directions + walk cycle), 10 PixelLab floor tiles.
- NPC brain: real job state -> work at desk / think / announce results / hand off to next agent; idle life -> coffee,
  chat pairs, arcade, rest, window gazing, sleeping at night; ZERO-1 services rooms with real problems.
- World: day/night from PC clock, simulated or real weather (Open-Meteo), city skyline with flying cars, rain/snow/storm.
- Server Room shows real CPU/RAM/GPU/VRAM/temperature (psutil + nvidia-smi).
- /api/world endpoint (agents, events -> speech, system metrics, issues, weather), AI-written NPC small talk when idle.
